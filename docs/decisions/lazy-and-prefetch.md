# Decision log: Lazy layouts and prefetch

Lazy lists and grids, item reuse, the policy of composing only what is in view, prefetch. Part of the [decision log](../decisions.md): the conventions and the index by number are there. The design documents describe how things work now; each row says what was decided, why, what was rejected, and links to the section with the detail.
