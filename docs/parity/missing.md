# What's missing for parity with Compose

A short overview, refreshed on 2026-11-07. The [ledgers](README.md) have the detail, and [handoff.md](handoff.md) says what is in flight and why each ignored test is ignored. Numbers are the Compose tests the ledgers count as waiting on a feature, tallied roughly: they show size, not exact remaining work, and some ledger tags lag behind what's done.

Out of scope, by decision: Material 2, Wear, TV, Glance, remote, Navigation Compose. iOS and WASM are paused.

## The big missing things

- **Touch text selection** (about 230; [design](design/touch-text-selection.md)): handles and selection gestures in text and fields. A state-based field scrolls by a remembered `ScrollState` by default, as Compose's (D2017). A finger's double tap on a field (D2018). - **Text context menu** ([design](design/text-context-menu.md)): its API, the toolbar handler and the platform toolbar's provider are in (D2031), a field's toolbar too (D2032, its review's fixes D2035: no autofill item until autofill exists), Android's floating toolbar through JNI (D2033), the dropdown's state and position (D2034), its items, popup, area and right click (D2036), the default dropdown provider (D2037), Compose's default providers' composable (D2038), the dropdown's position at placement (D2039), a field's right click (D2040), a container's toolbar and right click (D2041, D2042), the gesture ports' toolbar checks (D2043), a field's tap after a right click's first Down recorded as a difference (D2044), an open right-click menu's items following the field or the selection (D2045), a review's fixes to the container's toolbar (D2046); left: the content receiver's pastes (the autofill items' tests pass since D1684), **magnifier** (about 80), **handwriting** (about 85), **text links** (about 55).

### 2. Layout and composition foundations
- **The rest of lookahead:** the lazy layouts' own lookahead tests (`LazyStaggeredGridInLookaheadTest` is ported, D1318 to D1320, but for the dispose test's expectations that need `isCacheWindowLookaheadCheckEnabled`, an experimental flag off at tip, and so are 13 of `LazyGridTest`'s 16 lookahead cases (D1321; the others read an internal or need that flag); twelve of `LazyListTest`'s lookahead cases are (D1326), the others left being `needsToBePlacedInApproach` (what the pass that's now alone composes is placed ahead where it is now) and ones on an internal or that flag; the scope configuration of the placement-animation tests), now unblocked: lists, grids and staggered grids compose their items per item in either pass (D1312, D1316) and work in a scope (D1226: the pass that's now writes nothing they tell or keep, and `animate_item` places an item at its offset ahead, as Compose's; no `scrollDeltaBetweenPasses`); the `SubcomposeLayout` cases (PL-9, now that it's in); and the rest of `LookaheadScopeTest`: shared elements' tests, six on internal nodes to rewrite as scenes, two with movable content, and about a dozen still to port (direct manipulation, nested placement, skipped placement, `transformFrom`, a pager, a lazy list with animated items, the `align` form of the coordinates' consistency, `isLookingAhead` with intrinsics). The scope, the pass, `approach_layout`, `local_lookahead_position_of_with` (a scroll left out if asked, D1225), the intrinsics and baseline cases and the scene tests are in.
- **The public `LazyLayout`** (about 40 tests), now that lists, grids and staggered grids compose each item in a subcomposition during measurement, as Compose's (C8, D1312, D1316). Its design and four steps: [lazy-layout-api.md](design/lazy-layout-api.md); its four steps are in (`IntervalList`, `MutableIntervalList` and their 19 tests, D1322; `LazyLayout`, its item provider and measure scope over the lists' composer, D1323; `LazyLayoutPrefetchState`, D1324; `LazyLayoutPinnableItem` and `LazyLayoutPinnedItemList`, D1325; `LazyLayoutTest`'s 17 portable cases, `LazyLayoutStateRestorationTest` on it). Left: nested prefetch (`onNestedPrefetch`, `NestedPrefetchScope`), `LazyLayoutIntervalContent` and `LazyLayoutKeyIndexMap`, Compose's `LazySaveableStateHolder` (the state of keys not composed dropped when saved), and the lists' own prefetch and pinning moved onto the public types. Left of C8 with it: the window's kept items as precomposed handles, `precompose` in a policy and a never-measured precomposed slot, slot lookups, and the dead lacking-item paths.
- **Place-with-layer** is in (D1909, [design](design/place-with-layer.md)): `place_with_layer`, `place_relative_with_layer` and their `GraphicsLayer` overloads, with the nine layout-rect callbacks' tests that waited on it; `animate_item` places its children with the animation's layer, so what's before it in the chain moves and fades too (D1910). Still missing: the `zIndex` parameter of `place`, `LayoutModifierNode.updateLayerBlock`, recording into a `GraphicsLayer` placed with, and `offset_with` placing with a layer as Compose's (a move draws its content again here). `animate_item` doesn't tell lookahead scopes apart yet; its grid and staggered tests are partly ported.
- **Shared elements** (about 87; [design](design/shared-elements.md)): `SharedTransitionLayout`, the shared modifiers, the state machine and the overlay of recorded layers are in (D1329), all of `SharedTransitionTest` ported (D1332 to D1338; one n/a, a write from another thread), the moved element's detach and attach since D1923 and D1924. A compose-ui gap no test of it shows (D1338): a lazy item a scroll moves isn't told `on_placed`, where Compose tells each placement. And a `GraphicsLayer` drawn by several drawings shows in each as it was when that one drew it (D1337, D1339), where Compose's render node shows its last state in all. All of `DeferredSharedElementTest` too (D1340): a deferred content's manual transforms move its shared elements with it (drawn in the overlay, or in place in its layer), and the hand-off starts where they were left, at the gesture's velocity. Two visible shared elements of one key in a transition never settle, where Compose animates neither (D2190; ignored as review: in compose-navigation's `review_nav3_complete.rs`). **Still missing:** visual debugging. A compose-ui gap found on the way (D1341): a node that is both a layout and a draw node draws in its own coordinates, where Compose draws it in its content's (the coordinator below it), with its content's size; the shared node makes up for it in the overlay, other such nodes (custom ones recording their content into a `GraphicsLayer`, or reading their draw size) would differ from Compose's. `animateBounds` is in (D1328: all 8 of `AnimateBoundsTest`), on compose-ui's public `ApproachLayoutModifierNode`.
- **Android view interop**, `AndroidView` and hosting (about 280).
- **Modifier nodes moved by movable content** are detached and attached, as Compose's (D1923; in Compose's orders, content taken out of removed content too, and reading where it was, D1924); the tests of it about attach order, focus, hover, presses, `pointer_input` and insets are ported. ### 3. Graphics
- **`GraphicsLayer` as an object** is in (D1903): recorded, drawn by any drawing any number of times, its transform, alpha, clip and outline, shadow, color filter, blend mode and compositing strategy; copied into each drawing that draws it, so a drawing that drew it before it changed in a frame draws again, in a second pass of that frame (D1331), as Compose's render node is current at once. Still missing: its 3D rotations, camera distance and render effect, `GraphicsContext`, and recording a placement's content into a layer placed with (D1909: it gives its properties only).
- **Color spaces** are in (D1911, D1912): `Color` as Compose's 64 bits with its space, the `colorspace` package, conversions and connectors, Oklab `lerp`; Skia draws wide colors converted to extended sRGB, Android's replayer as color longs. Wide gradients go to Android as color longs (D1915); shadow colors and color filters are ARGB there, as Compose's (Android takes no long for them). Still missing: animating a color in its own space (`Color.VectorConverter(colorSpace)`), and a check on a wide-gamut phone screen.
- **Offscreen canvas and bitmaps** are in on the host (D1913): `ImageBitmap::new`, `Canvas` drawing into it at once, `CanvasDrawScope`, `read_pixels`, `PixelMap`, `GraphicsLayer::to_image_bitmap`, rasterized by Skia; drawings wait in the bitmap and are drawn in one pass when it's read, one canvas or thread at a time (D1914). On Android, the replayer draws them with a software `Canvas` over an `android.graphics.Bitmap` (D1915; built and linted, its bytes tested on the host, not run on a phone). `skew`/`concat`, clips out, `draw_points` and `draw_vertices` are in (D1916). Still missing: `ImageShader`, bitmaps kept in another config or space than 8-bit sRGB.
- **`Painter`** is in (D1901): `Painter`, `BitmapPainter`, `ColorPainter`, `Modifier.paint`, `Image(painter)` (`PainterImage`) and Material's `Icon(painter)` with a color or a `ColorProducer` tint. Still missing: `BrushPainter`, `FilterQuality`, and `painterResource`.
- **Blend modes and color filters** are in (D1900): `BlendMode`, `ColorFilter`'s tint, lighting and matrix, `ColorMatrix`, on paints, images and layers, on Skia and Android (checked on the phone with the demo's `graphics` tab: filters, blend modes, tinted icons and a vector). Still missing: `Brush.composite`, a blend mode on `drawImage`, and a graphics layer's `colorFilter` and `blendMode`.
- **Vector graphics** are in (D1902): `ImageVector` and its builder, `PathNode`, `PathParser`, `VectorPainter` with `remember_vector_painter`, groups with transforms and clip paths, a recorded drawing kept per size, `Image(imageVector)` (`VectorImage`), trim paths (D1908). Still missing: stroke joins and miters, vector composition (`Group`/`Path` composables and `rememberVectorPainter { }`), and **animated image vectors** (about 20).
- **Path geometry** is in (D1908): `PathIterator`, `PathMeasure`, `PathHitTester`, direction, division, reversal, SVG in and out, the path operators, `Path::copy`, computed in Rust (about 50 tests). Still missing: the geometry of a path whose operations the platform computes (Android, D535).
- **Blur** is in for a uniform radius (D1904): `Modifier.blur` with its edge treatments, a graphics layer's blur render effect, on Skia and on Android's render nodes (API 31+), at no cost to layers without one. Still missing: progressive blur (`BlurRadiusSpec`, about 60 tests), and blur on a `GraphicsLayer` object.
- **Drop and inner shadows** are in (D1905): `Modifier.drop_shadow`, `inner_shadow`, their painters, `shadow::Shadow` and `lerp`, drawn with a blur mask filter rather than offscreen bitmaps (overlapping drawings of one shadow in a layer, so it fades as Compose's one mask). A graphics layer drawn twice, bounded and unbounded blur and both shadows were checked on the phone (the demo's `graphics` tab). Still missing: their block overloads (`DropShadowScope`, `InnerShadowScope`) and `ShadowContext`.
- Brush extras, polygon shapes (about 65).

### 4. Foundation
- **`background` sets no semantics.** Compose's `BackgroundNode` is a `SemanticsModifierNode` setting `shape` and `backgroundColor` (for accessibility focus outlines); compose-rs's doesn't. 7 `BackgroundTest` tests are written and ignored (handoff.md's ignored tests).
- **Style API** is in (D1580 to D1587): properties, `CommonStyle`, states with the interaction source, the resolver and its node, the applying modifiers, animation between states, the content and text locals. Still missing: `StyleUxTaskTests`' remaining 24 tasks (styled buttons, switches, texts), the style layer's rotations about X and Y, camera distance and color filter, the appearance node's shape semantics. **Border parity** (about 80).
- **Overscroll's pixels:** `StretchOverscrollScreenshotTest` and `OverscrollTest`'s drawing tests have no port (a phone check stands for them). Lists and `vertical_scroll` clip both axes at rest, without `clipScrollableContainer`, so shadows across their axis are clipped where Compose's aren't.
- **Gestures and scrolling:** `transformable`'s trackpad pans and scales (7; ui has the events, D1674; the rest is in, D1369), foundation's `TrackpadScrollingLogic`, 2D scrolling's remaining tests (about 17: the semantics scroll during a scroll capture, the trackpad; D1370; the nested semantics scroll, a dispatcher inside and a cancelled child's fling are in, D1478), `bringIntoView` in the lazy layouts: they read the spec (D1317) but jump to what's brought into view (the other scrolling containers animate and keep a focused child in view when their viewport shrinks, as Compose's `ContentInViewNode`, D1376), public lazy layout API (about 40), focus search laying out items beyond a lazy layout's bounds through its `BeyondBoundsLayout` in the same frame (the lazy layouts answer it, D1479, but the search keeps its one-item-a-frame path, D477) and a grid laying out single items beyond its bounds rather than lines (D1479); a lazy layout's bring-into-view scroll reaching the nested-scroll nodes above it (the lazy layouts and `scrollable_2d` are in the tree's chain, D1478, but a lazy layout answers a bring-into-view at once, with the tree busy), and `NestedScrollModifierTest`'s 8 cases driven by a swiped or wheeled `LazyColumn`.

### 5. Material 3
- **Whole components missing:** carousel (about 100), floating toolbar (about 80), search bar (about 65), wide navigation rail (about 45), wavy progress (about 45), short navigation bar (about 35), loading indicator (about 20), FAB menu (about 15).
- **Done lately:** toggle buttons (D1570), the button group with its overflow (D1571), the split button (D1572).

### 6. Input and platform
- **Indirect pointer input** is in (D1675): the events to the focus's path, the cancels, the navigation gesture; left the Android host's touch-navigation events and about 20 tests (delegates, deeper trees). **Pointer icons** are in (D1676, D1677): `PointerIcon` with Android's types and bitmaps, `pointer_hover_icon_with(icon, override_descendants)`, a stylus's hover and `stylus_hover_icon`, the desktop's cursor and the Android view's (D1678, not yet run on a device); left `stylusHoverIcon`'s touch bounds expansion. Scroll capture (about 45), clipboard API (about 30), content capture, drag and drop, rotary input, media queries. **A pressed mouse leaving a node** hears the move and its `Exit` at the release, where Compose's `HitPathTracker` makes that move the node's `Exit` (each node of a hit path has its own `isIn`): `MouseEventTest.pressMoveRelease` ignored (handoff's ignored tests).
- **Locale and device data:** the date and time pickers use small tables for a few languages. Real data needs `icu4x`, a decision for the user.

### 7. The test framework
`compose-test` lacks parts of ui-test that ports will need: device configuration override, and capture to image. Trackpad injection (D1674: pans and pinches as Android 14 delivers them, on ui's pan and scale events) and indirect pointer injection (D1675) are in.

## Not verified on a device

Written and tested on the host, but not yet confirmed on a phone:

- **Autofill** (D1687, D1688, D1689): the Android host's bridge to `AutofillManager` builds and lints; the virtual structure, the notifications, filling and the switch following `AutofillManager.isEnabled` (read when the view gets its host and when its window takes the focus) haven't been tried with a service on a phone.
- **TalkBack's speech and announcements.** The accessibility tree was read directly, but no agent can enable a service.
- **A keyboard that really composes.** Gboard on the test phone doesn't compose, so long-lived composition hasn't run on a real keyboard (D1671).
- A real D-pad and a real mouse, rotation, voice typing.
- Android's real `RippleDrawable` (`feature:android-platform-ripple`).
- **Overscroll's glow** (API 29 and 30): Compose's code path, built and linted, tested on a fake only; the stretch was checked on API 37.

## Known costs against Compose's shape

- A focus change in a text field costs about 10 % more than before the decoration box work, and a keystroke about 5 %.
- Movable content moved to another applier (another host's tree) is new, its state not moved (D1511). Across compositions sharing a tree it moves as Compose's, composing again only under other locals; `Dialog` and `Popup` are in their caller's composition here, so moving into them keeps state.
- Movable content moved between two providers of an equal static value composes again, in a composition or across: what's provided is compared holder by holder, by identity, where Compose's `StaticValueHolder` is a data class compared by value (D1511, on review).
- A composite key token (what a layout node keeps for its hash, computed when focus asks, where Compose's `LayoutNode` keeps the hash) of content that moved to another composition is found by a look through the live compositions' named groups, not at once; and a token of a group `ReusableContent` reuses under another key reads the reused content's hash, where a hash Compose read before stays the old one (D1511, third review).
